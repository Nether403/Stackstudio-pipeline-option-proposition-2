## Overview

Set up your first automated development pipeline in StackStudio.

## Steps

<Steps>
  <Step title="Connect repository">
    ````bash
    Link your GitHub or GitLab repo from the Integrations panel.
    ````
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose template">
    Pick a pipeline template matching your stack or create a custom one.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Configure stages">
    Add build, test, and deploy stages as needed.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Run pipeline">
    Click "Run" to execute and monitor your pipeline.
  </Step>
</Steps>
